# The Sandwich

## Assumptions
- The bread, the peanut butter jar, and knife are all present.
- The bread has already been cut into slices.

## Steps
- undo the twist tie on the bread bag
- open the bread bag
- take bread out of the bag
- place bread on the table
- take the knife 
- slice the bread
- place the knife on the counter
- place one slices of bread on the plate
- open the jar of peanut butter
- remove the seal off of the jar of peanut butter
- take the knife
- put the knife in the jar of peanut butter
- take the knife
- scoop peanut butter with the knife
- spread peanut butter on the first slice of bread
- place knife on the counter
- place slice two on the plate

## Done When
- sandwich has been made
