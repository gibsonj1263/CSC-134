## Upstairs

> You climb to the upper floor. Cracked portraits line the long hallway.
> A soft voice whispers behind a bedroom door. The floorboards groan beneath your feet.

> 1. Open the bedroom door and investigate the voice

> Choice:

Investigate the voice:

> You open the door onto a dusty nursery scattered with broken toys.

> Zombies climb the stairs behind you. The mansion has found you.

Escape through the tunnel:
